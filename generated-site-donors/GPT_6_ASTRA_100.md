## Cross-corpus conclusion

Astra is substantially more design-/interaction-heavy than the Fable 5.1 corpus. Its value is therefore not raw donor count but a handful of **orthogonal primitives** Fable did not provide clearly: phyllotaxis, 4D projection, pressure-head hydrostatics, ballistic trajectories, proximity-graph wavefronts, weave topology, and a generic inverse-cube vector-field renderer. Overlaps should generally retain the stronger Fable implementation as the primary donor and keep Astra as an alternate/minimal reference.

## Verification rule

Do not validate a candidate against its own generated output. Promotion to a SHADED donor requires an independent reproducible acceptance test or trusted reference equation/fixture. For physical claims, verify dimensional consistency, conservation/invariants where applicable, and known limiting cases.
