## Important failures / false friends

### 093 `enigma-cipher-machine` — rotor-position math is broken

The page contains real historical rotor wirings, reflector B and plugboard state, but the final encode path applies rotor offsets incorrectly on the reverse pass. At zero rotor offsets the mapping is involutive, but at non-zero positions the same transformation generally does **not** decrypt itself. Keep the UI/state-machine idea, not the cipher math.

### 081 `stained-glass-cathedral` — recursive split is not robust polygon subdivision

The routine partitions existing polygon vertices by a horizontal/vertical cut but does not insert edge/cut intersection points. The result can fail to tessellate the source polygon correctly. Useful sketch, not a geometry oracle.

### 069 `prism-light-puzzle` — mirrors are real, prism dispersion is not

Ray/segment intersection, reflection and splitter branching are implemented. The prism, however, simply fans fixed hue rays by authored angle offsets; there is no Snell law, refractive index, interface normal or wavelength-dependent IOR.

### 059 `lava-lamp-lava` — thermal vocabulary, no fluid solver

Blobs carry a heat scalar and are pushed upward/downward heuristically. There is no pressure, continuity, advection, viscosity or buoyancy field solve.

### 032 `warming-stripes` — synthetic climate data

The page procedurally generates a warming-like time series with authored trend, noise and volcanic dips. It is not observation data and not a climate model.

### 086 `observatory-star-chart` — map projection only

The chart has pan/zoom projection and named objects but no RA/Dec→Alt/Az transform, sidereal time or ephemeris. Fable 097 remains the much stronger astronomy donor.

### 068 `gnomon-sundial-garden` — geometric approximation, not solar astronomy

Shadow length follows a `1/tan(altitude)`-style relation, but sun altitude/azimuth are authored from hour/season rather than computed from latitude/date/solar coordinates.

## Most SHADED-relevant takeaways

1. **057 is the standout.** It adds a real lattice/statistical-mechanics family that none of the earlier generated-site collections supplied cleanly.
2. **033 confirms GLM 5.3 can produce real numerical mechanics**, not just visual effects; it is independently useful for falsification against Muse's N-body implementation.
3. **071 is valuable because it is topology-first rather than graphics-first.** World relationships can be represented as a graph whose legal state transitions propagate through the graph.
4. **058 is boring in the best possible way.** Dimension strings, construction order and section views are useful editor/debug infrastructure that often matters more than another pretty shader.
5. **097 is a nice SHADED-style coupling pattern:** an environment field changes a mechanical state, which crosses a threshold and emits a persistent world event/audio consequence.
6. **GLM 5.3 is much stronger than GLM 5.2.** It still contains many presentation-first pages, but the best computational kernels are materially deeper and more varied.

## Verification policy

1. Page title/prompt is metadata, never evidence.
2. Extract the actual computational kernel and state its real contract.
3. Inspect initialization, buffer/state updates, force accumulation, topology and whether computed values actually affect output.
4. Mark visual approximations, synthetic data and false physical labels explicitly.
5. Deduplicate by primitive family, not by page theme.
6. Validate equations/units/invariants independently before promotion into SHADED.
7. For ports/regressions, use independent black-box scenes/metrics; never use the generated demo as its own golden oracle.
