## Important failures / false friends

### `011-solar-system` — not orbital mechanics
Planet positions are presentation orbits with authored periods/distances. There is no gravity, Kepler equation or N-body integration.

### `036-aurora-audio-rings` — not audio analysis
The page exposes frequency/amplitude-style controls but does not analyze an audio stream. It is a parameterized ring animation.

### `064-water-ripple` — not a wave solver
Each click creates independent expanding circle objects with radius/alpha/lifetime. There is no spatial heightfield, neighbor update or wave equation. Fable 099 / Qwen 27B 005 / DeepSeek 050 remain the real discrete ripple donors.

### `072-crystal-prism` — not optics
Beam and spectrum transforms are authored CSS rotations/scales. There is no Snell law, refractive index, surface normal or wavelength-dependent dispersion. Fable 082 remains the strong optics donor.

### `081-neural-net-viz` — graph activation, not a neural network implementation
It creates layered nodes with random weights, forwards decaying activation and animates packets, but there is no nonlinear layer computation, loss function, gradient/backprop or learning. Keep it as a network-signal visualization primitive, not ML.

### `092-plasma-ball` — procedural lightning, not plasma/electrostatics
The filaments are randomized branching curves aimed at pointer/ambient targets. No electric field, charge integration or plasma model is computed.

### `059-moon-phases` — useful geometry, not astronomy
The illuminated hemisphere mask is real geometric shading logic, but lunation is driven directly by a slider; there is no ephemeris or Sun/Earth/Moon orbital state.

### `056-physics-balls` — useful toy, not robust rigid-body dynamics
Circle collision response is compact and visually plausible, but masses are not physically derived and the impulse formula is an authored equal-mass-like approximation.

### Synthetic dashboards
`021-data-galaxy`, `024-hologram-ui`, `054-neon-dashboard`, `068-radar-scope`, `087-ticker-finance`, and `099-aurora-dashboard-pro` generate local/synthetic telemetry. Their rendering/state primitives may be useful; the data is not production/domain truth.

## Most SHADED-relevant takeaways

1. **044 is the real surprise.** Grok hides a representation-conversion primitive inside an ASCII art page: analytic geometry/fields are sampled and quantized into a density-symbol alphabet. That is more interesting than the page theme suggests.
2. **052 adds a compact discrete rules engine.** Not because SHADED needs chess, but because the pattern “state → legal transitions → history/undo” is reusable for rule-bound world interactions.
3. **068 is useful as an event-front pattern, not as radar physics.** A moving angular front detects contact with polar targets and leaves a decaying persistence trail.
4. **090 is a strong independent vector-field reference**, but it mostly reinforces families we already have rather than opening a new one.
5. **Grok is much stronger at algorithmic presentation/authoring than at physical simulation.** Its natural-phenomenon titles frequently overstate the underlying computation.
6. **The collection again validates the full-scan rule:** `ascii-portrait` and `chess-arena` are more valuable computationally than many files whose names explicitly advertise physics, water, optics or plasma.

## Verification policy

1. Page title/prompt is metadata, never evidence.
2. Extract the actual computational kernel and its real contract.
3. Check whether named physical quantities are actually computed rather than just exposed as UI labels.
4. Mark synthetic data, visual approximations and false physical labels explicitly.
5. Deduplicate by primitive family, not page theme.
6. Validate equations/units/invariants independently before promotion into SHADED.
7. For ports/regressions, use independent black-box scenes/metrics; never use the generated demo as its own golden oracle.
