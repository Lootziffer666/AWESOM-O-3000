## Important failures / false friends

### 041 `prism-spectrum` — no physical dispersion

The page labels bands by wavelength, but the fan angle is authored directly from band index and incidence-angle heuristics. There is **no Snell law, refractive index, interface-normal calculation or wavelength-dependent IOR**. Fable 082 remains the real optics donor.

### 066 `caustic-garden` — caustic look, no caustic computation

The bright patterns are animated radial gradients and wavy rings. No reflected/refracted ray family is traced and no intensity focusing is computed.

### 073 `interference` — additive rings, not a wave-interference field

Sources emit expanding circles and Canvas additive blending creates moiré-like overlaps. The code never samples and sums wave amplitudes over the domain, unlike Qwen Max 059.

### 023 `orrery` — prescribed angular motion

Planet angle is simply `phase + simulatedDays / period * 2π`. This is an orrery animation, not gravity, Kepler integration or N-body mechanics.

### 018 `pendulum-wave` — prescribed oscillator family

Each bob uses a fixed period and direct `sin(phase)` displacement. It is a phase/synchronization visualization, not a pendulum ODE solver.

### 060 `liquid-blobs` — blob dynamics, not fluid dynamics

Centers are spring-attracted toward moving targets and rendered as fused blobs. No fluid pressure/advection/continuity solver is present.

### 092 `wave-tank` / 081 `paper-boat` — analytic wave surfaces, not water PDEs

Both compose sine waves and Gaussian transient impulses. Objects follow the resulting surface/slope, which is useful, but the surface itself is prescribed.

### 016 / 027 / 085 — synthetic dashboards

Trading, polar-weather and flight instrumentation are local/generated state, not production data or domain models.

## Most SHADED-relevant takeaways

1. **093 is the clean surprise.** It is hidden inside a brutalist 404 page, yet contains a real perfect-maze generator and BFS solver. This validates the “never scan by title” rule again.
2. **048 adds a missing discrete fire family.** It should be treated as a cellular heat/fire automaton, not combustion chemistry, but it is useful for fast stylized propagation and cross-backend tests.
3. **069 is conceptually valuable because particles change a persistent surface field.** That maps directly to SHADED's “effects alter world state” principle: leaves are not merely sprites once they land; they contribute to the ground/pile representation.
4. **065 is a compact event-network primitive.** A world system can treat wires/relations as topology and propagate bounded signals through them.
5. **The independent overlaps are valuable for falsification.** Ripple, Gray-Scott, Marching Squares and Chladni now have more independent implementations, which is useful for cross-checks without adopting any generated demo as the golden oracle.
6. **Qwen 27B is clearly weaker than Qwen Max in raw simulation depth**, but it has a surprisingly good ratio of compact world-state and authoring primitives.

## Verification policy

1. Generated page title/prompt is metadata, never evidence.
2. Extract the actual computational kernel and state its real contract.
3. Inspect initialization, buffer swaps, force/state accumulation, boundary conditions and whether computed values actually influence output.
4. Mark visual approximations, synthetic data and false physical labels explicitly.
5. Deduplicate by primitive family, not by page theme.
6. Validate equations/units/invariants independently before promotion into SHADED.
7. For ports/regressions, use independent black-box scenes/metrics; never use the generated implementation as its own golden oracle.
